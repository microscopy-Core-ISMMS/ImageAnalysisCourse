## AI use declaration

These workshop materials — slide decks, lab notebooks, build scripts, figures, and accompanying handouts — were developed with substantial assistance from **Anthropic's Claude** (Sonnet and Opus model families) accessed via the **Cowork research-preview** desktop interface during workshop development (April–May 2026).

**How AI was used.** Drafting slide and notebook markdown; authoring build scripts (`build_figures.py`, `build_lecture_notebook.py`, the per-notebook builders) and dataset bake / patcher utilities; writing the matplotlib code that produces all synthetic lecture figures and the four interactive demo cells in Lecture 1; project bookkeeping (deliverable tracking, audit logs, handoff documents); typesetting and brand-asset application on the Day 2 Fiji handout.

**What was not done by AI.** Pedagogical content selection, topic scope, lecture flow, and the choice of lab exercises were the instructor's. The Day 2 Fiji content derives from prior human-authored course materials (BSR2601 and the Mt Sinai Fiji set), with AI used only for restructuring, typesetting, and brand application. Final review, approval, and live delivery are human-only.

**Verification.** Citations in the lecture bibliography carry an inline flag — ✓ verified via web search this session, ✏ corrected from training-knowledge, ⚠ uncertain, ❓ author synthesis. Entries marked ⚠ have not been cross-checked against published source articles and should be confirmed before redistribution. The verification audit trail for Lecture 1 lives in `lectures/01_intro_ai_imaging/verification_log.md` (linked under **Extras** in the sidebar).

**Known limitations.** AI can fabricate plausible-sounding citations, dates, and statistics. Where flagged, this risk is mitigated; for unflagged passages, treat factual claims as needing independent verification. The synthetic figures in the lecture illustrate concepts — they are not data and should not be cited as scientific evidence.

**Why this disclosure.** Open AI-use disclosure is the responsible-use norm recommended in Lecture 1, Section 7 (Reproducibility). This page applies the same standard to the workshop materials themselves.
