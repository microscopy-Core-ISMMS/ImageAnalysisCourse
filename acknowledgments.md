## Attribution

The methods, datasets, and tools used in the lab notebooks are the work of the bioimage-analysis community. Brief attributions:

- **Cellpose-SAM** — Pachitariu, Rariden, Stringer, *bioRxiv* 2025; original Cellpose family from Stringer, Wang, Michaelos, Pachitariu, *Nature Methods* 18:100–106, 2021. [Cellpose GitHub](https://github.com/MouseLand/cellpose).
- **Segment Anything (SAM)** — Kirillov et al., *ICCV* 2023. [SAM GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything).
- **μSAM (micro-sam)** — Pape et al., *Nature Methods* 2025. [μSAM GitHub](https://github.com/computational-cell-analytics/micro-sam).
- **Noise2Void** — Krull, Buchholz, Jug, *CVPR* 2019. [n2v GitHub](https://github.com/juglab/n2v).
- **CARE** — Weigert et al., *Nature Methods* 15:1090–1097, 2018. [CSBDeep GitHub](https://github.com/CSBDeep/CSBDeep).
- **StarDist** — Schmidt, Weigert, Broaddus, Myers, *MICCAI* 2018. [StarDist GitHub](https://github.com/stardist/stardist).
- **ZeroCostDL4Mic** — von Chamier et al., *Nature Communications* 12:2276, 2021. [ZCD4M GitHub](https://github.com/HenriquesLab/ZeroCostDL4Mic).
- **DL4MicEverywhere** — Hidalgo-Cenalmor et al., *Nature Methods* 2024. [DL4MicEverywhere GitHub](https://github.com/HenriquesLab/DL4MicEverywhere).
- **BioImage Model Zoo** — community model registry. [bioimage.io](https://bioimage.io).
- **QuPath** — Bankhead et al., *Scientific Reports* 7:16878, 2017.
- **Trainable Weka Segmentation** — Arganda-Carreras et al., *Bioinformatics* 33:2424–2426, 2017.
- **scikit-image** — van der Walt et al., *PeerJ* 2:e453, 2014.
- **Fiji** — Schindelin et al., *Nature Methods* 9:676–682, 2012.
