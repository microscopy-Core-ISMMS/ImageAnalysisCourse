Attribution#

The methods, datasets, and tools used in the lab notebooks are the work of the bioimage-analysis community. Brief attributions:

  • Cellpose-SAM — Pachitariu, Rariden, Stringer, bioRxiv 2025; original Cellpose family from Stringer, Wang, Michaelos, Pachitariu, Nature Methods 18:100–106, 2021. Cellpose GitHub.

  • Segment Anything (SAM) — Kirillov et al., ICCV 2023. SAM GitHub.

  • μSAM (micro-sam) — Pape et al., Nature Methods 2025. μSAM GitHub.

  • Noise2Void — Krull, Buchholz, Jug, CVPR 2019. n2v GitHub.

  • CARE — Weigert et al., Nature Methods 15:1090–1097, 2018. CSBDeep GitHub.

  • StarDist — Schmidt, Weigert, Broaddus, Myers, MICCAI 2018. StarDist GitHub.

  • ZeroCostDL4Mic — von Chamier et al., Nature Communications 12:2276, 2021. ZCD4M GitHub.

  • DL4MicEverywhere — Hidalgo-Cenalmor et al., Nature Methods 2024. DL4MicEverywhere GitHub.

  • BioImage Model Zoo — community model registry. bioimage.io.

  • QuPath — Bankhead et al., Scientific Reports 7:16878, 2017.

  • Trainable Weka Segmentation — Arganda-Carreras et al., Bioinformatics 33:2424–2426, 2017.

  • scikit-image — van der Walt et al., PeerJ 2:e453, 2014.

  • Fiji — Schindelin et al., Nature Methods 9:676–682, 2012.

A full bibliography lives in lectures/01_intro_ai_imaging/references.bib.
